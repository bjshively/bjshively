# Hey, I'm Brad 👋

By day, I'm a Developer Experience engineering director in the self-driving vehicle industry, where I've spent the last 10 years making complex systems more accessible to developers. By night, I build indie iOS apps under [blacknoise.ai](https://blacknoise.ai) — thoughtful, user-focused tools that solve real everyday problems.

## What I'm Working On

### 🐾 [Pet Pilot](https://blacknoise.ai/petpilot)
A pet activity tracker designed for households — coordinating feeding, walks, medications, and more across family members. Built with SwiftUI and CloudKit for seamless sharing.

### 🧘 [Ease](https://blacknoise.ai/ease)
An anxiety tracking journal that helps you understand patterns in your mental health over time. Privacy-first, with all data stored locally on your device.

### 📍 [StopBy](https://blacknoise.ai/stopby/)
Save places you mean to visit and get a reminder when you're nearby. It can also find stops along a planned route.

### 🧳 [Wear Next](https://blacknoise.ai/wear-next/)
Plan outfits for each day of a trip from your own closet, then build a packing list from what you plan to wear.
