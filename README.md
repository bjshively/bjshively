## What I'm Working On

A pet activity tracker designed for households — coordinating feeding, walks, medications, and more across family members. Built with SwiftUI and CloudKit for seamless sharing.

### 🧘 [Ease](https://blacknoise.ai/ease)
An anxiety tracking journal that helps you understand patterns in your mental health over time. Privacy-first, with all data stored locally on your device.
